About me

I am Icarus, a cybersecurity engineer and security researcher focused on blockchain protocols. I review Solidity and Rust codebases, turn issues into reproducible Foundry, Anchor or Rust tests, and work with teams from discovery through remediation.

My work spans DeFi, DAO, DLT and hardware-related systems across EVM, Solana and Move ecosystems. I also build security tools and automated research workflows in Rust and Python.

Before Web3 security, I spent three years in the public sector working on networks, user support, EDR deployment, security audits, ISO 27001 and a full cyber-crisis exercise. That operational background still shapes how I assess real systems: understand the trust boundaries, challenge assumptions, validate impact and communicate clearly.
